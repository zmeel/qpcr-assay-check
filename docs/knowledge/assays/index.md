# Assays

The example assays: where the sequences come from and what was checked.

* [Entamoeba histolytica, SSU rRNA (one FAM-MGB probe)](entamoeba-histolytica-ssu.md) - One primer pair and an MGB probe in the 18S rRNA gene; variants from about 2,345 Nucleotide rRNA records because only 13 genome assemblies exist.
* [Enterovirus (realT, in-house) RT-qPCR](enterovirus-realt.md) - In-house 5' UTR assay with two forward primers, a degenerate reverse primer and an MGB probe; human enteroviruses, not rhinoviruses.
* [Giardia lamblia, SSU rRNA (Verweij 2004 multiplex)](giardia-lamblia-ssu.md) - One primer pair and a linear FAM/BHQ1 probe in the 18S rRNA gene, 63 bp; the supplied reference sequence was the three oligos concatenated, so the real amplicon was taken from NCBI instead.
* [HSV-1 detection, glycoprotein G (Ryncarz 1999 typing oligos)](hsv1-gg-typing.md) - The laboratory's HSV-1 detection assay of about 20 years, built from the paper's typing oligos - type-common primers in gG (US4) with the HSV-1-specific probe, 166 bp; checked against the paper's PDF. It detects HSV-1 only - HSV-2 gives a product without a signal. The inosine in the reverse primer is written N.
* [HSV-2 detection, glycoprotein D](hsv2-gd.md) - The laboratory's HSV-2 PCR, companion of its HSV-1 assay - one primer pair and a FAM/TAMRA probe in gD (US6), 71 bp; source unknown. All three oligos are type-specific (5, 4 and 3 mismatches against HSV-1). Oligo QC without a single flag.
* [Influenza A, matrix gene (Erasmus MC Viroscience protocol)](influenza-a-matrix.md) - One forward primer, two reverse primers and two linear FAM/BHQ1 probes in segment 7; the second reverse primer covers the 3'-terminal escape the runs found.
* [Legionella genus + L. pneumophila](legionella-genus-pneumophila.md) - One primer pair on the 23S-5S spacer with a genus probe (VIC) and an L. pneumophila probe (FAM); the example in the repository is a draft with placeholders.
* [Neisseria gonorrhoeae, two MGB probes](neisseria-gonorrhoeae-two-probes.md) - One primer pair with two alternative FAM-MGB probes on a multi-copy opa region; sequences supplied by the user.
* [VZV, ORF38](vzv-orf38.md) - One primer pair and a 26-nt probe with one degenerate base in ORF38 (tegument protein UL21), 82 bp; oligos supplied without a publication. The probe's G form, matching the Oka lineage, folds into a hairpin (Tm 73.6 C).
