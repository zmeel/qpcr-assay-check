# What we take from it

- The MGB folds into the minor groove of the terminal 5-6 bp (p. 655) and can slide 1-2 bp toward the 5' end (pp. 657, 661).
- T/G under the MGB: dTm 15 vs 6 C, ddG 5.6 vs 2.0 kcal/mol without MGB (p. 657); 7 nt from the 3' end dTm 11 vs 6.5 C (p. 660).
- A 12-mer with a mismatch 5 nt from the 3' end gave no meaningful signal on the mismatched template from 55 to 70 C (Fig. 7, p. 659).
- 11 nt from the 3' end: dTm 8.5 C with and without MGB (p. 661).
- A/C at the terminal base and C/A at position 6 discriminated less, unexplained by the authors (p. 657).
