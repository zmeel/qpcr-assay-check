# What we take from it

- The swH1 probe is an unmodified 30-mer with an internal BHQ1 quencher, not MGB (Table 1, footnote b, p. 92).
- Every sample was detected (swH1 Ct up to 38.53, p. 92); the theory review's 'clinical false negatives' overstates it.
- Two probe mismatches plus reverse-primer mismatches: mean Ct gap to InfA 9.28 vs 5.58 with one probe mismatch; homologous oligos recovered 4.59 Ct (Table 3, p. 93).
