# Sources

Papers, reviews and NCBI documentation the rules and facts rest on.

* [Bru et al. 2008 (Appl Environ Microbiol)](bru-2008.md) - One internal primer mismatch can distort quantification up to 1000-fold; the detection-not-quantification caveat.
* [Christopherson et al. 1997 (Nucleic Acids Res)](christopherson-1997.md) - Internal primer-template mismatches in RT-PCR of HIV-1 RNA; the RNA note in the report.
* [Elbrecht et al. 2018 (Sci Rep)](elbrecht-2018.md) - Primer slippage across homopolymers changes amplicon length; cited for R5b.
* [Herpers et al. 2003 (J Clin Microbiol)](herpers-2003.md) - Real-time PCR on the 23S-5S spacer for Legionella spp. and L. pneumophila; the published source of the user's Legionella assay.
* [Howson et al. 2020 (Pathogens) - GoPrime](howson-2020.md) - GoPrime in silico framework with 90 mismatched templates; named by the advisor as independent validation data.
* [Huang et al. 1992 (Nucleic Acids Res)](huang-1992.md) - Single-step extension kinetics of terminal mispairs by Taq; C-T the most easily extended.
* [Klungthong et al. 2010 (J Clin Virol)](klungthong-2010.md) - Primer and probe mismatches in a pandemic H1N1 assay; reduced sensitivity, no false negatives, unmodified probe.
* [Knight et al. 2025 (Sci Rep)](knight-2025.md) - Machine-learning prediction of mismatch impact on PCR; named by the advisor as independent validation data.
* [Kutyavin et al. 2000 (Nucleic Acids Res)](kutyavin-2000.md) - 3'-MGB probes: a mismatch under the minor groove binder is far more destabilising; basis of the 7-nt MGB region in R9.
* [Kwok et al. 1990 (Nucleic Acids Res)](kwok-1990.md) - PCR yield for every terminal primer-template mismatch; terminal G2 and G3 amplified like a match.
* [Lefever et al. 2013 (Clin Chem)](lefever-2013.md) - Mismatch position beyond the last 5 nt, counts per primer and per pair; basis of R2, R3 and R8.
* [NCBI BLAST Common URL API](ncbi-blast-urlapi.md) - The documented BLAST URL API - parameters, report formats, word sizes and usage guidelines.
* [NCBI Datasets v2 OpenAPI specification](ncbi-datasets-openapi.md) - NCBI's published OpenAPI spec for Datasets v2, the source of the endpoints and parameters the genome download uses.
* [NCBI E-utilities documentation](ncbi-eutils.md) - Entrez Programming Utilities - ESearch, ESummary, EFetch and their usage rules.
* [Otwell et al. 2025 (Front Cell Infect Microbiol)](otwell-2025.md) - Wet-lab Ct shifts for 132 mismatched DNA templates of 16 SARS-CoV-2 assays; the calibration set for R3b, R5c and section 11.
* [Theory review by the methods advisor (2026-10-01)](review-advisor-2026.md) - Independent review by the methods advisor (diagnostic qPCR, quality systems), kept verbatim.
* [Theory review by a molecular biologist (2026-10-01)](review-molecular-biologist-2026.md) - Independent review of the tool's theoretical basis by a senior theoretical molecular biologist and bioinformatician, kept verbatim.
* [Stadhouders et al. 2010 (J Mol Diagn)](stadhouders-2010.md) - Single primer-template mismatches in the last 5 nt by type, position and PCR setup; Table 1 is the R1 lookup.
* [Süss et al. 2009 (J Microbiol Methods) - not obtained](suss-2009.md) - Single mismatches in primer and probe binding regions; could not be obtained, so the unmodified-probe rule stays expert judgement.
* [Tanaka et al. 2004 (Biochemistry)](tanaka-2004.md) - Nearest-neighbour parameters for DNA with a single-bulge loop; cited for R5b.
* [Ye et al. 2012 (BMC Bioinformatics) - Primer-BLAST](ye-2012.md) - Primer-BLAST; the word-size blind spot and the 30,000 E-value default the report compares against.
* [Zhu and Wartell 1999 (Biochemistry)](zhu-wartell-1999.md) - Stability of single-base bulges in DNA and RNA; cited for R5b.
