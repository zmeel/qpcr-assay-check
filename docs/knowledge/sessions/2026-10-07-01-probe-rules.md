# 2026-10-07: Probe rules

- User doubted R5c ("hard to believe that a 1-5 nt deletion in a probe is only at risk") and asked
  for an advisor literature search. The advisor searched PubMed and Europe PMC and parsed Otwell's
  supplementary workbook. Findings: a phrase search for a probe-site deletion returns that one
  study; its whole dataset is 15 templates from 4 assays; of the nine points with 1-5 nt deleted,
  eight are one assay (C4 ORF8) and **2 nt and 5 nt were never tested**; all probes are 25-28 nt
  linear ZEN/IBFQ at 55 C over 50 cycles, so **nothing covers MGB or short probes**; at 6 nt the
  study holds two near-identical geometries with opposite outcomes; the failure mode is signal
  loss (minimum positive RFU 14,000-27,000 -> ~2,900 at 6 nt with only +4 Ct), not amplification
  loss; every real-world case (TaqPath S-gene dropout and the like) is 6 nt. No evidence was found
  to move the thresholds either way.
- User then read the Legionella run of 2026-10-02, "Needs attention (104 combinations)", row 2
  (7 L. micdadei genomes, GCF_000953635.1): the unmodified 35-nt LEGpneu probe with **11
  mismatches and 2 gaps** graded `at_risk`, next to the 19-nt MGB LEGgenus probe with 7 mismatches
  graded `likely_failure`. Cause: R9's unmodified branch had no count-based escalation, so
  everything past one internal mismatch fell into one `at_risk` fallback; the gap handling (R5)
  then kept that class.
- User's decision (asked, two readings were possible): **2 mismatches at risk, 3 or more likely
  failure**, for unmodified probes; MGB unchanged. Folded with R5c into one change.
- Built: `UNMODIFIED_PROBE_FAIL = 3` in `oligo/grade.py` with the Klungthong note on the 2-step;
  `_r5c_scope()` and `UNTESTED_DELETIONS = {2, 5}` add scope flags to deletion notes without
  changing any class. Tests in tests/test_grade.py (the ladder, and that the notes say when a
  probe length, chemistry or deletion length was never measured). Docs: MISMATCH_CLASSES R9 and
  R5c, the report's class paragraph, the wiki page, and the bundle's R9, R5c and a decision page.
- No change to any headline figure: at risk and likely failure both count as not detected. Only
  Legionella has unmodified probes among the examples, so only its rows change.
